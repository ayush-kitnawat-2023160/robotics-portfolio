THERE'S MORE TO ME
THAN MY GITHUB.

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at IIIT Delhi. What excites me most about engineering is seeing ideas come to life beyond a computer screen. There's something particularly satisfying about taking a rough idea, turning it into code, connecting a few pieces of hardware, and watching it actually do something in the real world.

I also enjoy the research side of things. I like diving into papers, understanding how others have approached problems, and exploring what happens when I combine ideas from different places. Of course, things rarely work the way I expect them to. I've spent my fair share of time chasing bugs, questioning assumptions, and figuring out why something that made perfect sense on paper refuses to work in practice. But that's part of the fun for me — experimenting, trying different approaches, and occasionally stumbling upon something that works surprisingly well. Either way, I usually end up learning something I hadn't set out to learn.

Away from the keyboard, I enjoy cooking, playing cricket and basketball, watching anime, photography, and trekking. I like exploring different interests, trying new things, and occasionally doing absolutely nothing productive. After all, there's more to life than debugging code.
