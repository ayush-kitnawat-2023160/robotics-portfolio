I'm Ayush Kitnawat, a computer science undergraduate at IIIT Delhi who loves building robots and figuring out how they work.

My projects span robotic arms, 3D reconstruction, multi-camera tracking, embedded systems, and autonomous navigation. What connects them is my interest in giving machines the ability to perceive their surroundings and do something useful with that information.

I enjoy working across the whole stack — understanding the math, writing the code, connecting sensors, and testing things on actual hardware. Outside engineering, I enjoy cooking, photography, and sports.
